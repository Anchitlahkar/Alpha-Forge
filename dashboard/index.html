GenFirst: Stable End-to-End Latent Generative Modeling

8.45Ranked

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers · signal 8.0/10

Researchers introduced GenFirst, a 'generation-before-reconstruction' strategy that enables stable end-to-end training of latent generative models, achieving state-of-the-art results in image and multimodal generation by addressing latent collapse.

So what

Directly training latent generative models without collapse becomes possible. This simplifies the development of unified multimodal generation systems.

Key points

  • Latent generative models typically use a two-stage pipeline, training a variational autoencoder for reconstruction then a generative model on the frozen latent space.
  • Direct end-to-end training of these models is challenging due to latent collapse and a generation-reconstruction conflict.
  • GenFirst, a simple generation-before-reconstruction strategy, achieves the first direct end-to-end training without latent collapse.
  • The method is based on two insights: entropy in the Kullback-Leibler divergence prevents collapse, and reconstruction and generation have asymmetric learning dynamics.
  • GenFirst first shapes the latent space with a generative objective under weak reconstruction pressure, then progressively strengthens reconstruction to recover visual details.

Worth doing

  • Read the paper arxiv:2608.29335 to understand the GenFirst strategy in detail.
  • Look up the authors, starting with Yiyuan Zhang, to see their other contributions.
  • Visit the project page linked from the Hugging Face paper page for additional resources.

On-Policy Self-Adaptation: Improving Language Model Reasoning Without Teacher Supervision

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers

On-policy distillation (OPD) improves language model reasoning by suppressing unlikely tokens. Teacher guidance is not its main driver. A new method, On-Policy Self-Adaptation (OPSA), uses the model's own uncertainty to achieve better results without a teacher.

  • On-policy distillation (OPD) suppresses low-probability tokens. Teacher guidance is not its main driver.
  • Teacher supervision in OPD contains substantial noise, but student performance is insensitive to it.
  • A fixed negative advantage can match the performance of teacher-provided advantages in OPD.
