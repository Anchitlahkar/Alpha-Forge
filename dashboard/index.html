So what

A new mathematical framework unifies quantum information concepts. This could provide a more coherent understanding of quantum states and their dynamics.

Key points

  • The Quantum Information Space at Infinity (Quinfinity) $\mathcal{Q}_\infty$ is introduced as an inverse limit of symbolic Quantum $N$-Spaces $\mathcal{Q}_N$.
