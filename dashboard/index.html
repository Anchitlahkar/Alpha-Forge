PhysBrain 1.5 Embodied AI Model

9.22Ranked

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers · signal 9.0/10

PhysBrain 1.5 is a new open-source embodied AI model that integrates physical understanding, action generation, and future state prediction. It achieves state-of-the-art performance, comparable to proprietary models, by unifying these critical aspects.

So what

A single model can now understand physical environments, generate actions, and predict future states, matching proprietary systems in open-source performance. This advances the development of general-purpose embodied AI agents.

Key points

  • PhysBrain 1.5 is a unified model for understanding physical environments, generating actions, and predicting future states.
  • It uses joint autoregressive training on discrete vision-language, motion, and visual target sequences.
  • The model's 8B version scored 72.5 on average across 28 embodied understanding benchmarks.
  • It sets a new open-source state of the art and performs on par with proprietary models like GPT-6-Astra and Gemini 3.6 Flash.
  • PhysBrain 1.5 achieves the best open-source results on 14 benchmarks while keeping general multimodal capabilities.

ZGCM-1 Open Foundation Model for Math and Agentic Search

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers

ZGCM-1 is a new 7B open foundation model that demonstrates exceptional efficiency and performance in math and agentic search tasks, matching much larger frontier models. Its efficiency (4.2x training improvement) is attributed to architecture-system co-design, progressive context scaling, and an AI-native R&D workflow managed by agent swarms.

  • ZGCM-1 is a 7B open foundation model designed for math and agentic search, trained from scratch with high data, system, and algorithmic efficiency.
  • It combines internal reasoning with external tool use, allowing compact models to overcome parametric capacity limits.
  • The model uses an end-to-end, high-efficiency training recipe, including interleaved gated sliding-window and full attention, and a stable FP8 Muon optimizer.
