DiffusionART: Efficient, Stable and Scalable Reinforcement of Diffusion Models

8.45Ranked

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers · signal 8.0/10

DiffusionART is a new method for fine-tuning diffusion models with reinforcement learning, offering significantly improved efficiency and stability compared to previous approaches.

So what

Training diffusion models with reinforcement learning can now be much faster and more stable. This makes it easier to develop high-quality image and video generation systems.

Key points

  • Online reinforcement learning (RL) fine-tuning of diffusion models faces efficiency issues with reverse-process methods like Flow-GRPO and instability with forward-process methods like DiffusionNFT.
  • DiffusionART (Diffusion Reinforcement with Aggregation-First Reward Tilting) aggregates each prompt's rewards into a shared reward-improving direction for reverse-process states.
  • It removes a hidden forward-reverse consistency force found in NFT-style objectives and adds a trust-region-masked second update for drift-adaptive restoration without needing Exponential Moving Average (EMA) tuning.
  • The method requires no differentiable reward model, works effectively with fewer samples per prompt, and supports pairwise reward models.
  • On Hunyuan Video-13B, DiffusionART achieves the same late-stage mean reward levels as DiffusionNFT and Flow-GRPO-Fast with 12.02 and 11.72 times fewer GPU hours, respectively.

Worth doing

  • Read the paper arxiv:2610.12572 on Hugging Face.
  • Clone the GitHub repository linked on the paper page to explore the code.
  • Look up the authors, Zhenglin Wan and Yang You, for their other work.

SoftwareGym: Scaling Computer-Use Agent Training with Diverse Synthesized Software Environments

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers

SoftwareGym is an automated pipeline that synthesizes diverse, runnable GUI software environments for training computer-use agents (CUAs), improving their performance on benchmarks like OSWorld.

  • Computer-use agents (CUAs) are improving, but synthesizing diverse and reliable software environments at scale remains a challenge.
  • SoftwareGym is an automated pipeline for synthesizing GUI software environments with diverse interfaces, executable functionality, and runtime-verified behavior.
  • It generates structurally diverse designs, realizes them as runnable applications, and uses GUI evaluation with iterative repair to ensure functional reliability.

VisionWeave: Weaving Elastic Visual Representations as a Native Capability of MLLMs

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers

VisionWeave introduces a new method for Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) to adaptively process visual information, saving 43% of visual tokens and increasing throughput by 2.3x while maintaining 98.9% performance.

  • Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) currently incur high costs by encoding visual inputs into dense, fixed-size patch tokens.
  • Visual information is unevenly distributed, requiring varied levels of detail.
  • VisionWeave introduces "elastic visual representation weaving," a native MLLM capability to learn where and at what granularity to allocate visual representations.
