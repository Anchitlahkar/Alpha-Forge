Motion-Omni: Joint Speech and Full-Body Motion Generation

8.45Ranked

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers · signal 8.0/10

Motion-Omni is an end-to-end AI framework designed to jointly generate spoken dialogue and corresponding full-body motion from shared hidden states. This framework achieves real-time, aligned responses, operating 5.4 times faster than previous cascade methods.

So what

AI systems can now generate both speech and realistic full-body motion simultaneously and efficiently. This enables more natural and responsive virtual agents and digital humans.

Key points

  • Motion-Omni is an end-to-end framework that jointly generates spoken dialogue and full-body co-speech motion.
  • It replaces the traditional cascade approach, which generates speech then motion, allowing for joint optimization.
  • The system uses co-adapted LLM, Speech Generator, and Motion Generator components.
  • Supervision comes from a scalable pseudo-labeling pipeline with 422,856 quality-ranked pairs.
  • Motion-Omni-Q7, using a Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct backbone, responds 5.4 times faster than real time (RTF=0.78).

Optimizing Layer Dropout for LLMs

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers

New research demonstrates that layer dropout, a technique previously abandoned for Large Language Models (LLMs), can significantly reduce training FLOPs by up to 25% or improve validation loss. When applied with optimal configurations, it can also speed up inference by 1.5 times.

  • Layer dropout, also known as stochastic depth, was largely removed from LLM pre-training recipes due to reported accuracy degradation.
  • This study shows that with optimal configuration, layer dropout leads to lower loss at the same training FLOPs or saves up to 25% training FLOPs for similar validation loss.
  • It enables up to 1.5x inference speedup through post-training optimizations like early exit, intermediate-layer skipping, and self-speculative decoding, with negligible accuracy loss.

Iris Search Agent Development

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers

Researchers have developed Iris-mini and Iris-pro, two open-source search agents that achieve state-of-the-art results on complex web benchmarks. These agents utilize a multi-stage SFT-RL (Supervised Fine-Tuning and Reinforcement Learning) climbing training pipeline and advanced inference-time context management.

  • Iris-mini and Iris-pro are two search agents trained at 35B-A3B and 397B-A17B scales.
  • They use a multi-stage pipeline combining supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL) against live search.
  • The training procedure, called SFT-RL climbing, alternates SFT and RL, returning the hardest solved and most efficient rollouts from RL to the next supervised pass.
