Fold2Reason: Protein Folding for General AI Reasoning

8.52Ranked

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers · signal 8.0/10

A new AI model, Fold2Reason, was developed and post-trained on protein folding data. This model significantly improved both protein structure prediction and general reasoning across 10 different benchmarks, suggesting that learning protein folding can generalize to broader reasoning tasks.

So what

AI models can learn broader reasoning capabilities by training on structured scientific data like protein folding. This suggests that complex scientific problems can serve as a rich, non-linguistic source of supervision for improving AI.

Key points

  • Large language models often lack spatial and structural logic found in scientific problems.
  • Protein folding offers a natural testbed, providing thousands of checkable spatial and topological statements from one solved structure.
  • Researchers created FoldingCorpus, a protein-derived question-answer dataset.
  • They developed Fold2Reason, a post-training method that uses discrete structural answers and continuous 3D geometry.
  • Fold2Reason achieved structure prediction scores 2.7 to 3.5 times higher than Qwen3.5-9B on FoldBench.

Counterdiabatic Quantum Circuits Theory

Quantum Computing · arXiv quant-ph

Researchers developed a Hamiltonian-free theory for counterdiabatic driving. This advancement enables faster quantum control for Floquet systems and quantum circuits without the necessity of a local effective Hamiltonian.

  • Counterdiabatic driving, a method for fast quantum control, typically requires a Hamiltonian generator.
  • This research introduces a Hamiltonian-free theory for the adiabatic gauge potential (AGP) for parameter-dependent unitary operators.
  • The exact and regularized AGP versions come from a Hermitian, banded linear system.

MotorMind: Zero-Shot Robot Manipulation with VLMs

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers

Researchers developed MotorMind, a system that enables general vision language models to perform zero-shot robot manipulation with high success rates on both simulated and real robots, without requiring task-specific training or external tools.

  • MotorMind is a robot manipulation harness that connects VLM-proposed mid-level actions to deterministic robot control and feedback.
  • It uses asynchronous monitoring and background memory updates.
  • The system does not require task-specific policy training, coding agents, or additional grounding tools like SAM3.
8.38 World Embedding Benchmark for Video Representations

Researchers introduced the World Embedding Benchmark, a dataset comprising 8,000 simulated video-text cases across 80 physical families. This benchmark evaluates how effectively video representations capture physical information, revealing a trade-off between cross-modal alignment and quantitative physical information recoverability in pre-trained models. However, it also demonstrated that physics-adapted models can improve retrieval-augmented video generation.
