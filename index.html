The Clifford Hierarchy from Clifford Stabilizers in 2D

9.15Ranked

Quantum Computing · arXiv quant-ph · 20 Nov · signal 9.0/10

Researchers found a way for 2D topological quantum codes to implement complex logical gates at any level of the Clifford hierarchy, bypassing a known theoretical limit. This uses non-Abelian group multiplication in the encoding.

So what

New methods allow topological quantum codes to perform a wider range of operations. This expands the design space for fault-tolerant quantum computers.

Key points

  • The Bravyi-König theorem limits constant-depth logical gates of 2D topological Pauli stabilizer codes to the Clifford group.
  • Previous qubit codes with Clifford stabilizers only exceeded this limit by one level of the Clifford hierarchy.
  • New research shows this bound can be completely bypassed.
  • Topological codes with Clifford stabilizers in 2D can now realize transversal logical gates at any level of the Clifford hierarchy.
  • The paper presents two explicit constructions based on quantum doubles of D4 and Z2.

Mind2Act World: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Embodied Robotic Manipulation

AI Research · Hugging Face Papers

Researchers introduced Mind2Act World, a diagnostic benchmark with 180 configurations for robotic manipulation, revealing that current AI models fail to compose history-based reasoning and precise execution, despite competence in individual skills.

  • Mind2Act World is a new diagnostic benchmark for embodied intelligence in robotic manipulation.
  • It features 30 simulated manipulation tasks across five real-world domains, with 180 configurations.
  • The benchmark evaluates three views: Mind (history-based reasoning), Act (physical execution), and Mind2Act (composition of both).
